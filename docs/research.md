# Research ledger

Confirmed facts about real z/OS behaviour that the sim models (or chooses not to), each with its source and how
the sim stands against it. This is the place to look up "is this how z/OS really works?" and the place to record a
new finding.

- **SPEC §17** rates each model's fidelity and points here for the facts behind it. **SPEC §18** is the
  bibliography. **PROGRESS.md "Open questions"** holds only what is still unsettled; once a question is settled,
  its finding moves here. **BACKLOG.md list `realism-fixes`** holds the model changes queued by entries below.
- Page citations give the PDF page index, labelled, with the printed page in parentheses: `PDF p.201 (printed 183)`
  (CLAUDE.md "Realism policy"). Links open the same index (`#page=N`). Printed numbers were read from each page's
  footer; on a page with no footer (a full-page table) they come from the book's offset. Patent PDFs have no printed
  page numbers. Books are the z/OS 3.1 editions unless noted. Redbooks are point-in-time snapshots by their authors; the edition date is given, and
  the z/OS book wins where both cover a fact.
- **Checked** = the page was read and says what the row claims. **Web** = cited from a web page or practitioner
  source; not checked against a book page.

Sim status:
- **Matches**: the sim does what the source says.
- **Approx**: same mechanism, simplified; the difference is stated. Code carries `// APPROX:`.
- **Fix queued**: the sim differs and a change is queued in BACKLOG.md list `realism-fixes` (or, for WLM policy
  adjustment, the named WT entry of list `wlm-truth`; design in [wlm-design.md](wlm-design.md)).
- **Not modelled**: real behaviour the sim leaves out, on purpose.
